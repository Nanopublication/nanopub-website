Here are a few examples of published nanopublications:

- [`RAlohixCBW`](https://w3id.org/np/RAlohixCBWPuGLSKA7mmR8k01EQvC_dRWwAU70kvvwKPo)
  is linking a species of ground beetles to a habitat type
- [`RAjcIFuVe7`](https://w3id.org/np/RAjcIFuVe7RvBHB35oZ4RN5G_L5-EDm4jCz0xuw2clr0Q)
  is stating a scientific claim as an AIDA sentence and linking it to more specific claims
- [`RACK9s48ZO`](https://w3id.org/np/RACK9s48ZO9wtNaYNScBuwNuP9H5IvYKiFHzqYRd6CGtw)
  is expressing a testable hypothesis in developmental biology, together with a proposed test
- [`RAn4ChpN8n`](https://w3id.org/np/RAn4ChpN8nXUYA_z1gDSMA_pN95nm5CmmKksIpOLM3L74)
  is formulating a research question in the PICO format (population, intervention, comparison, outcome)
- [`RA2hQWbV8l`](https://w3id.org/np/RA2hQWbV8lg9-ST37Hpt4cGT69wcQUpuNj94EBFtuP0go)
  is introducing and defining a new concept ("generalist registry")
- [`RAV_-_t50g`](https://w3id.org/np/RAV_-_t50gbCnKkbkaAj_BlMDVwR-e2ilVN_0ztbd4FNo)
  is defining a scientific variable as a FAIR Digital Object (FDO), following the I-ADOPT framework
- [`RAsyWOaZ7W`](https://w3id.org/np/RAsyWOaZ7W23n9z0IfCozt89ZHJ1ydUyo5XRQgLQxV_Gg)
  is stating how three papers cite a given paper, with typed citations (CiTO)
- [`RAkDzG4wAG`](https://w3id.org/np/RAkDzG4wAGHllfIFZl7C60OTOMHL9U5bOj790TYkkdhBQ)
  is highlighting a passage of a scientific article and commenting on it
- [`RAbp6LX5SR`](https://w3id.org/np/RAbp6LX5SR11W2_LDzV66rq6-gdJOLybxoBhqdW1EkPVM)
  is stating that the creator has read a particular paper
- [`RACEKjxyGN`](https://w3id.org/np/RACEKjxyGNkonER8LJo-VIBGxNPYEqW3kNgb3XA4t_kxQ)
  is declaring that the creator participates in a conference
- [`RASg5YBHps`](https://w3id.org/np/RASg5YBHpsSUMkLa4RVy55dTDZL9XA-Q3F-lqDIc9Jm7Q)
  is introducing a user and declaring the public key they sign their nanopublications with
- [`RALC35Q-V7`](https://w3id.org/np/RALC35Q-V73jQ7zb4QYFIhXzpNYEQLrozZsQMcQYfFoYo)
  is defining a template, from which nanopublications like the AIDA sentence above are created
- [`RATC_tCEzO`](https://w3id.org/np/RATC_tCEzOumv1KQ-sIeiB62qJ5BljQ5PBSLvRtVLRSQ0)
  is defining a SPARQL query, which is then available as an API
- [`RAVa2aRzyu`](https://w3id.org/np/RAVa2aRzyubhbw1la-gPdTlcdyJziS_FbeWwGGbhkPM2I)
  is declaring the retraction of another nanopublication
