These are published nanopublications.
Each link opens a readable view in your browser.
To get the RDF instead, request the same URI with `Accept: application/trig`, as shown in the [quick start](/docs/).

## Scientific knowledge

- [`RAlohixCBW`](https://w3id.org/np/RAlohixCBWPuGLSKA7mmR8k01EQvC_dRWwAU70kvvwKPo) links a species of ground beetle to a habitat type.
- [`RAjcIFuVe7`](https://w3id.org/np/RAjcIFuVe7RvBHB35oZ4RN5G_L5-EDm4jCz0xuw2clr0Q) states a scientific claim as an AIDA sentence and links it to more specific claims.
- [`RACK9s48ZO`](https://w3id.org/np/RACK9s48ZO9wtNaYNScBuwNuP9H5IvYKiFHzqYRd6CGtw) expresses a testable hypothesis in developmental biology, together with a proposed test.
- [`RAn4ChpN8n`](https://w3id.org/np/RAn4ChpN8nXUYA_z1gDSMA_pN95nm5CmmKksIpOLM3L74) formulates a research question in the PICO format (population, intervention, comparison, outcome).
- [`RA2hQWbV8l`](https://w3id.org/np/RA2hQWbV8lg9-ST37Hpt4cGT69wcQUpuNj94EBFtuP0go) introduces and defines a new concept ("generalist registry").
- [`RAV_-_t50g`](https://w3id.org/np/RAV_-_t50gbCnKkbkaAj_BlMDVwR-e2ilVN_0ztbd4FNo) defines a scientific variable as a FAIR Digital Object (FDO), following the I-ADOPT framework.

## Scholarly communication

- [`RAsyWOaZ7W`](https://w3id.org/np/RAsyWOaZ7W23n9z0IfCozt89ZHJ1ydUyo5XRQgLQxV_Gg) states how three papers cite a given paper, with typed citations (CiTO).
- [`RAkDzG4wAG`](https://w3id.org/np/RAkDzG4wAGHllfIFZl7C60OTOMHL9U5bOj790TYkkdhBQ) highlights a passage of a scientific article and comments on it.
- [`RAbp6LX5SR`](https://w3id.org/np/RAbp6LX5SR11W2_LDzV66rq6-gdJOLybxoBhqdW1EkPVM) states that the creator has read a particular paper.
- [`RACEKjxyGN`](https://w3id.org/np/RACEKjxyGNkonER8LJo-VIBGxNPYEqW3kNgb3XA4t_kxQ) declares that the creator participates in a conference.

## The network itself

The nanopublication ecosystem is described in nanopublications too: users and their keys, the templates for creating nanopublications, the queries behind the API, and retractions.

- [`RASg5YBHps`](https://w3id.org/np/RASg5YBHpsSUMkLa4RVy55dTDZL9XA-Q3F-lqDIc9Jm7Q) introduces a user and declares the public key they sign their nanopublications with.
- [`RALC35Q-V7`](https://w3id.org/np/RALC35Q-V73jQ7zb4QYFIhXzpNYEQLrozZsQMcQYfFoYo) defines a template, from which nanopublications like the AIDA sentence above are created.
- [`RATC_tCEzO`](https://w3id.org/np/RATC_tCEzOumv1KQ-sIeiB62qJ5BljQ5PBSLvRtVLRSQ0) defines a SPARQL query, which is then available as an API.
- [`RAVa2aRzyu`](https://w3id.org/np/RAVa2aRzyubhbw1la-gPdTlcdyJziS_FbeWwGGbhkPM2I) declares the retraction of another nanopublication.
