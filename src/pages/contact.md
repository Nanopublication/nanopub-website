# Contact

- **Chat**: ask questions and discuss with the community in the public Matrix channel [#nanopub:matrix.org](https://matrix.to/#/#nanopub:matrix.org).
- **Mailing list**: subscribe to [nanopub-users](https://groups.google.com/g/nanopub-users/) for announcements and invitations to the Nano Sessions.
- **Online meetings**: join the [Nano Sessions](/sessions), which are open to everyone.
- **Mastodon**: follow [@nanopub@mas.to](https://mas.to/@nanopub).
- **Code and issues**: see the [Nanopublication](https://github.com/Nanopublication) and [Knowledge Pixels](https://github.com/knowledgepixels) organizations on GitHub.

## About

nanopub.net is managed by the Nanopublication Association, a non-profit organization based in Zurich, Switzerland.
You can reach it at [association@nanopub.net](mailto:association@nanopub.net).
